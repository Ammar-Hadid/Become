# Historical Entrepreneurship audit scope

The September 2026 audit inspected the active exploration overview before the authenticated session expired. Other routes were inspected in source and tests. The screenshot in `screenshots/01-exploration-overview.png` records that implementation, not a new design baseline.

Domain distinctions retained from the audit:

- Sources retain revisions, audience context and traceability.
- Conversation observations retain their source interaction.
- Problems preserve supporting and contradicting evidence.
- Follow-ups, decisions and source records preserve history.
- Outreach, no-contact attempts, conversations and learning evidence have distinct meanings.

The previous visual/UX judgments and recommended journey have been removed. See `docs/design/ENTREPRENEURSHIP_EXPERIENCE.md` for current capabilities.
