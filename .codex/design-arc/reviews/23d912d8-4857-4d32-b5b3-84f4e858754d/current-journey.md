# Current journey audit

- Status: partial live audit; benchmark review complete from official product guidance
- Product: Become Entrepreneurship
- Surface: responsive web, inspected on desktop
- Entry inspected: active exploration overview
- Primary objective: make the path through an exploration immediately clear
- Screenshot: `screenshots/01-exploration-overview.png`

## Current journey

1. Enter an exploration.
2. Move between Overview, Audience, Evidence, Problems and Offers.
3. Capture conversations, observations and public sources through `Keep evidence`.
4. Record outreach and replies through the interaction model.
5. Convert observations into problem evidence and record assessments.
6. Create and test offers.
7. Revisit follow-ups, time and direction decisions from the overview.

## Confirmed strengths

- Exploration context stays visible and the active tab is marked.
- Sources retain revisions, audience context and traceability.
- Conversation observations remain nested under their source interaction.
- Problems preserve supporting and contradicting evidence rather than forcing a positive conclusion.
- Follow-ups, decisions and source records retain historical truth.

## Confirmed structural friction

- The overview gives recent evidence, next action, audiences/problems/offers, activity metrics, time and decisions similar visual weight. It inventories the exploration but does not lead the user through it.
- `What are you learning?` promises synthesis while the page supplies a chronological record and counts. The user must perform the synthesis mentally.
- The main navigation is entity-based. It explains where records live, not what the entrepreneur should do next.
- Every `Interaction` is admitted to the Evidence journal. Because interaction kinds include outreach sent, reply received, conversation and no-contact attempt, activity and learning are semantically mixed.
- The same interaction system acts as outreach log, conversation source, reply chain and evidence container. Filters reduce the list but do not clarify those roles.
- B2C, B2B and both/unknown contexts can be filtered, but there is no first-class comparison or synthesis of what is known in each market.
- The overview's single follow-up is a recorded choice, not a reasoned next step based on the largest current uncertainty.
- The user can trace a conclusion downward to records, but cannot climb upward from records to a concise, reviewable current understanding.

## Evidence limits

- The active production overview was captured and inspected before the authenticated session expired.
- Remaining routes were inspected in current source and tests, not captured as live screenshots in this run.
- Mobbin benchmark access is blocked because the connected account reports that a paid plan is required. The benchmark set therefore uses current official product documentation from Linear, HubSpot, Intercom and Dovetail rather than Mobbin flow captures.
- The recommendation is benchmark-informed, but no visual-comparison claims are made where only documented product behavior was inspected.
