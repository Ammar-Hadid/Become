# Historical Entrepreneurship domain contracts

The former design directions and journey recommendations have been removed. Current capabilities are documented in `docs/design/ENTREPRENEURSHIP_EXPERIENCE.md`; inspect the owning implementation for current state.

- Observed facts, user interpretation, hypotheses, questions and decisions remain distinguishable and source-linked.
- Messages have explicit speaker identity, time and order. Historical summary-only conversations must not acquire invented transcripts.
- Messages become observations only through deliberate selection; observation support/contradiction remains attributable.
- Guides belong to a user and exploration. Draft concurrency is separate from immutable published revisions; a conversation retains the revision used.
- Content and Entrepreneurship keep separate persistence and authorization while sharing genuinely generic editor capabilities.
- AI proposals are reviewable and never silently rewrite accepted understanding or a pinned guide revision.
