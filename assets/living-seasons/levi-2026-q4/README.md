# Levi Ackerman · Living Season draft

This is a local demo-account art pack for Season 4, 2026. It has seven chapters, three progress states per chapter, and independently composed desktop and phone images for each state (42 images total). `reference-v1.png` anchors Levi's face, clothing, and the workshop setting. `review-desktop.jpg` and `review-mobile.jpg` show the full state matrix for review.

The continuous setting is a Survey Corps rooftop planning workshop above a walled city. On-track scenes show the workshop becoming orderly and operational; neutral scenes retain unfinished work; far-behind scenes show a specific repair backlog and a restrained, concerned Levi. Chapter changes and the character's expression, pose, and lighting change together. The portrait images were composed separately so his face and upper body remain visible on a phone.

Files follow `chNN-<on-track|neutral|far-behind>-<desktop|mobile>.png`. The local seed at `src/features/dashboard/testing/services/levi-living-demo-seed.service.ts` imports these into the disposable `demotest@gmail.com` account as a **draft**. Approval and activation belong to the Living Season editor's review flow.

Profile grounding uses the [official anime character listing](https://shingeki.tv/season1/character/), [episode 19 synopsis](https://shingeki.tv/season1/story/episode_19.php), and [official Levi cleaning special synopsis](https://shingeki.tv/news/archives/3094). The images are generated interpretations for the local demo; the cited pages are characterization references, not image sources.
