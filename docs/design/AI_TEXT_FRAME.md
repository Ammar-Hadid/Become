# AI text frame

The owner explicitly approved `/ai-glow-border-candidate` on 11 October 2026 after requesting that its box fit the text instead of spanning the screen. The approved treatment is now `AiTextFrame` in `src/components/ui/ai-text-frame/`. Storybook shows **AI → Text Frame** (Season Summary, Paused, Phone, Short Text) and **Documentation → Approved Metadata → Ai Text Frame**.

The primary-gold edge, softly tinted solid Card, outer and inner glow, and eight-second orbit match the reviewed candidate. The frame fits its content with a maximum 72ch reading column, stays within its parent on phones, and uses the shared action padding with dense padding on phones. Edit the grouped `--ai-text-frame-*` tokens in `src/app/styles/tokens.css` to tune approved values. Keep the enclosing page full width.

At the owner's request, the preview's approved Sparkles heading and identity line are shared through optional `heading`, `description`, and `headingLevel` props. The onboarding summary uses “AI-prepared Season summary” followed by its current model name, source work and adaptation. No character defaults live in the shared component. The heading labels its article automatically unless native naming attributes are supplied. Level 3 fits beneath the onboarding section's existing level-2 heading; preview pages use level 2. Storybook includes a second identity to demonstrate that the line is supplied by its consumer.

The component takes native article attributes, `children`, and `paused` (false by default). Name it through `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. Supply an adjacent shared pause/play Button with `aria-pressed` and `aria-controls` whenever animated, or choose a static frame with `paused`. All prose and links render immediately. The decorative edge cannot intercept clicks, imply model activity, verify accuracy, or delay approval and saving. Feature-owned headings, provenance, citations, corrections and approval actions stay outside the shared implementation.

Motion pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs. Reduced motion removes the animation while keeping the gold reading surface and native interaction. The visibility observer and tab listener are cleaned up on unmount. This is an explicitly approved decorative reading treatment, separate from loading or typing.

Both the glow review route and guided Season onboarding summary consume the shared component. After the owner approved Living Ink on 11 October 2026, they compose the frame with `AiGeneratedText` for longer AI prose and `type-body-lg` typography. Paragraphs reveal strictly in order through the approved gold trail. Numbered citations link to a compact feature-owned Sources row immediately below. The adjacent Pause motion / Resume motion Button controls both the border and text, preserving reading progress. There is no “Show full summary” button. Reduced motion displays the entire summary; the reveal never blocks corrections, evidence access or approval. Storybook's **With Generated Text** demonstrates this composition. `AiTypingText` is exclusively for C.O.T.S. speech. See [AI_GENERATED_TEXT.md](AI_GENERATED_TEXT.md). Neither shared component owns approval state.

This approval admits only the AI text frame; the full guided Season onboarding candidate remains under owner review and has not replaced the production opening.
