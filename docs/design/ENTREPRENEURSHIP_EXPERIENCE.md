# Entrepreneurship implementation contracts

This document describes implemented capabilities. Navigation names and screenshots in the verification ledger identify the audited implementation, not the product owner's new frontend design.

## Domain and implementation contracts

- History, explicit corrections and money qualification remain authoritative.
- Sources, observations, interpretations, questions and decisions retain their distinct meanings and provenance. Citation counts do not establish confidence.
- Nexo proposes; users review, edit, accept or discard. Accepted editions preserve sources and prior understanding.
- Audience records link outreach, conversations, evidence and offers. Outreach activity does not itself prove learning or contact.
- Collections search their complete owned scope before keyset pagination; selected records are fetched directly.
- Guide documents preserve prose and optional question blocks. Published revisions are immutable; conversations retain the revision used.
- Direct access to exploration capabilities never requires completing an earlier stage.

Shared token, lifecycle and reduced-motion contracts are in [MOTION_SYSTEM.md](MOTION_SYSTEM.md).

## Coverage ledger

The implementation and verification cover these reachable families, including populated, empty, archived, error and responsive states where applicable:

- Exploration launcher, scope/lifecycle, direct workspace navigation and legacy redirects.
- Current understanding, findings, hypotheses/questions, source trails, corrections and editions.
- Audience people/businesses/segments, membership, capture, editing and archives.
- Outreach activity, reply chains, batch capture and follow-up completion/rescheduling.
- Conversation start, transcripts, promotion to evidence, correction, pause/resume and debrief.
- Guide drafts, whole-document autosave, conflicts, publishing, pinned conduct and archives.
- Direct/public/historical evidence capture, observation attribution and withdrawal.
- Problem statements, assessment basis and source review.
- Specific offers, audience tests, terms/revisions, recipient outcomes and receipts.
- Finance and session entry points, contextual return URLs and global history.

The detailed [coverage and verification ledger](ENTREPRENEURSHIP_VERIFICATION.md) records destinations and evidence.
