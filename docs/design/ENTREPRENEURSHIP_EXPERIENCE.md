# Entrepreneurship experience

Approved September 22, 2026. Direct workspaces replace the former stage rail: Overview, So Far We Know, Audience, Outreach, Conversations, Evidence, Offers. The workflow is discover → contact → learn → interpret → decide, but navigation never requires completing an earlier stage. Existing history, correction and money qualification remain authoritative.

## Design contract

Keep charcoal/amber and the owner-controlled semantic typography scale. Use aligned compact record rows, intentional working space and one primary action. Optional explanations, historical detail, provenance and advanced fields are disclosed on request. No oversized introductory mastheads, readiness scores, citation-based confidence rankings or mandatory wizard.

Conversations show collection, transcript and context above 1240px, disclose context at intermediate widths, and use separate list/detail views at 800px and below. Whole guide documents preserve prose and optional question blocks. Collections search their complete owned scope before keyset pagination; selected records are fetched directly.

Canonical motion is defined solely in [MOTION_SYSTEM.md](MOTION_SYSTEM.md). Values are Become design choices calibrated against production guidance, not competitor measurements.

## Coverage ledger

The implementation and verification cover these reachable families, including populated, empty, archived, error and responsive states where applicable:

- Exploration launcher, scope/lifecycle, direct workspace navigation and legacy redirects.
- Current understanding, findings, hypotheses/questions, source trails, corrections and editions.
- Audience people/businesses/segments, membership, capture, editing and archives.
- Outreach activity, reply chains, batch capture and follow-up completion/rescheduling.
- Conversation start, transcripts, promotion to evidence, correction, pause/resume and debrief.
- Guide drafts, whole-document autosave, conflicts, publishing, pinned conduct and archives.
- Direct/public/historical evidence capture, observation attribution and withdrawal.
- Problem statements, assessment basis and source review.
- Specific offers, audience tests, terms/revisions, recipient outcomes and receipts.
- Finance and session entry points, contextual return URLs and global history.

The detailed [coverage and verification ledger](ENTREPRENEURSHIP_VERIFICATION.md) records destinations and evidence. Public reference research and local rendered verification are distinct activities.
