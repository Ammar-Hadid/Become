## Resume the blocked Finance tests

Use your disposable acceptance-test user for invented receipts. The fixtures used for this implementation are removed; they are not sample records in your application.

1. Open **Entrepreneurship → Market** at `/entrepreneurship/market`, then select the company. Its route is `/entrepreneurship/market/companies/{companyId}`.
2. In **Work relationship**, choose **Create / Link WorkSource**. Under **WorkSource action**, select **Create a WorkSource** and review **New WorkSource name**, or select **Link an existing WorkSource** and choose **Existing WorkSource**. Click **Confirm WorkSource connection**. The relationship appears on the company page. Creating/linking does not attribute old income.
3. If continuing from an acceptance, open `/entrepreneurship/offers/{offerId}`. **Record outcome** lets you select the exact **Quoted revision** and record **Accepted**. Review the prominent **Accepted terms**. In **Continue into work**, choose **Choose how to continue**; set the appropriate **Next step** and click **Save choice**. **Not now** and **No work-source link needed** are valid saved choices.
4. To use money already recorded, go directly to step 5. Otherwise, under **Actual receipts**, click **Record receipt in Finance ↗**. This opens `/finance/transactions?company={companyId}` with `&offer={offerId}` when applicable. Choose **Add transaction**, confirm **Income**, the intended account, amount/date and **Business revenue** or **Paid work** inflow classification, then click **Record transaction**. The normal Finance work-source selector is available under **Link work source or opportunity**. Click **Return to attribute receipt ↗** after saving.
5. Back under **Actual receipts**, choose **Attribute existing receipt**. Select **Finance receipt**, inspect the remaining amount, set **Amount to attribute**, and choose **Proposed offer revision · optional** or **Company only**. Click **Confirm attribution**. Click **View the Finance receipt ↗** to verify that Finance displays the same transaction and its business allocation.
6. Repeat attribution for deposits/installments or to split a receipt across offers. The remaining amount decreases; exceeding it is rejected. On the Finance transaction detail, use **Correct attribution** to withdraw an allocation, **Review attribution** after a material receipt correction, or the existing **Corrections** controls to edit/delete the receipt. Withdraw or correct allocations first if changing currency, eligibility, or reducing the receipt below its allocations.

## Backend and data safety

No migration was needed. The changes use existing CompanyWorkSource, observation revisions, offer events and Finance allocations. Backend additions are limited to company-link entry points, reusing atomic observation persistence for outcomes, resolving batch overrides, canonical search and correct read projections. Monetary parsing, Finance ownership, transaction locking, reconciliation, transfers, savings and recurring logic remain intact.

A before-task source snapshot was used to check preservation. Prisma schema, the active timer provider/controller and the Engineering project form remain byte-for-byte unchanged from that snapshot. The session-details provider received only the required capture-route/overlay coordination correction. Tests delete only their own registered users and dependent fixtures; no account balances or records belonging to the real user were changed.
